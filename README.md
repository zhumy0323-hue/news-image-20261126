Built for the [make.fun bounty "Sum up 26 November's world news in one image"](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261126) ($100).

A single 1024x1024 front page — THE THURSDAY PAPER — that sums up
Thursday 26 November 2026 in world news: 36 stories across 7 continents
and 16 fields.

## What is on the page

- **3 lead stories** — the day's biggest headlines, up top, each with
  its own illustration: Russia's own figures showing nearly 500,000 dead
  in the Ukraine war, North Korea ordering the Swiss diplomatic office
  shut within 48 hours, and Lebanon opening a second airport at Kleiat.
- **10 small stories** — each with its own illustration and a
  verification badge (CONFIRMED / OFFICIAL / REPORTED): RSF drone
  strikes in Sudan, Bangladesh's 36% garment wage rise, the killing of
  a senior Hamas security chief in Gaza, Ramaphosa's Farmgate court win,
  the Houthi strike on the Ras Tanura refinery, Malaysia suing Google,
  the Cartagena hostage rescue, Xi's graft probe of Chen Jining, US
  tariffs of up to 25% on Indian goods, and PIA moving UK flights to
  its own fleet.
- **23 more in brief** — a wire list of the rest of the day, kept
  short so every headline fits on one line.
- **UTC timeline** — 7 first-report times in order, from 04:26 to
  14:32 UTC.
- **Figures strip, region bar, and a world map** with 14 numbered pins
  placing the stories on the globe.
- **DATE CHECK box** — states what the page covers (26 November only)
  and what it deliberately leaves out as earlier news: Wednesday's
  Shenzhou-24 launch, the Trump tariff probe, the Poland drone
  incursion, and Tuesday's school bomb threats and Iran missile warning.

Every story is in the 26 November 2026 daily summaries
(India/Asia-Pacific outlets whose local date is 26 November),
cross-checked against Reuters, AP, AFP, BBC, UN News, TOI, Al Jazeera
and RFE/RL, and none of them was used in the 25 November entry. The
image was built at about 07:00 CST on 26 November, before the UTC day
began, so the timeline shows first-report UTC times, which fall on
25 November UTC. 26 November 2026 is a Thursday; it is Constitution
Day in India and the 18th anniversary of the 26/11 Mumbai attacks.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This decodes `entry.png.b64` and writes the 1024x1024 PNG to
`/output/entry.png`. Standard library only, no dependencies.
